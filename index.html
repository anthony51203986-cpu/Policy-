Privacy Policy

Information We Collect

Account Information: Email address, username, profile picture (if provided)

Chat Data: All conversations with Cecilia, including messages, file uploads, and image descriptions

Usage Data: Feature usage frequency, response times, error logs

Device Information: Browser type, operating system, device model, IP address

How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and improve Cecilia's responses
  • To train and fine-tune AI models (anonymized)
  • To troubleshoot technical issues and fix bugs
  • To comply with legal obligations

Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored securely using Firebase Firestore and Firebase Storage. We use encryption for data in transit (TLS) and at rest. However, no method of transmission is 100% secure.

Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • Firebase (Google): Authentication, database, storage
  • Groq: AI processing for chat responses
  • Tavily: Web search functionality
  • Mistral AI: Alternative AI processing
  • Wolfram Alpha: Math calculations
  • OpenRouter: Vision model for image analysis

Data Retention

Your chat history is retained until you delete your account. File uploads are stored until the associated chat is deleted.

Your Rights

  • Access: View all your data
  • Correction: Update your profile information
  • Deletion: Delete your account and all associated data
  • Export: Download your chat history

Children's Privacy

Cecilia is not intended for children under 13 years old. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with data, please contact us.

Cookies

We use localStorage to save your preferences (like font size, voice selection, and theme). No tracking cookies are used.

International Data Transfer

Your data may be processed on servers located in the United States and Europe. By using Cecilia, you consent to this transfer.

Changes to Privacy Policy

We may update this policy. We will notify you of significant changes via email or in-app notice.
